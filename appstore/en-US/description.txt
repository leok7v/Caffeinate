Caffeinate keeps your Mac awake. Click the cup in the menu bar and your Mac stays awake. Click it again and it may sleep as usual.

Right-click the cup for the options:

- Prevent display sleep
- Prevent system idle sleep
- Prevent disk idle sleep
- Prevent system sleep on AC power
- Prevent sleep with the lid closed, for a MacBook on battery with no external display (off by default)
- Auto-off timer: 15 minutes to 8 hours, or any number of minutes
- Start at login

The first four options match the flags of the built-in caffeinate command. A countdown shows next to the cup while the auto-off timer runs.

Caffeinate collects no data, makes no network connections, and shows no ads. It is free and open source under the MIT license.
