Caffeinate is a menu bar app (LSUIElement, no Dock icon). Left-click the cup icon in the menu bar to turn it on or off. Right-click the cup for the menu.

While on, it holds IOKit power assertions (IOPMAssertionCreateWithName), the same ones as the caffeinate(8) command.

The menu item "Prevent sleep with lid closed (on AC power)" is off by default. When the user turns it on and Caffeinate is active, the app calls IOConnectCallScalarMethod on IOPMrootDomain with kPMSetClamshellSleepState, a selector defined in the public SDK header IOKit/pwr_mgt/IOPMLibDefs.h. It uses no private symbols and works inside the App Sandbox. The option applies only on AC power. The app clears the setting when the user turns Caffeinate off, unplugs the power, quits, or relaunches. The auto-off timer can limit how long the Mac stays awake.

To test the lid option: on AC power with no external display, check "Prevent sleep with lid closed (on AC power)", left-click the cup so it fills, close the lid for a minute, and open it. The Mac did not sleep.

The app collects no data and makes no network connections. No account is needed.
