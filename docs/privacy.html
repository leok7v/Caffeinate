Privacy Policy

Caffeinate for macOS. Effective 2026-11-18.

Caffeinate collects no data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no accounts and no third-party code. The app makes no network connections.

What stays on your Mac

Caffeinate stores its settings (which sleep types to prevent, the auto-off timer, and start at login) in a small file inside its own sandbox container on your Mac. The file never leaves your Mac, and deleting the app removes it.

What the app does

While active, Caffeinate asks macOS not to sleep, through the power management interfaces of macOS. It reads nothing about you, your files or your activity.

Children

Caffeinate collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.
